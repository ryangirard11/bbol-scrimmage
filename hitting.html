Backyard Baseball 2001 · competitive mode

Hitting cheat sheet

Decoded from the online competitive patch scripts. Every number here comes from the game's own hitting code. Speeds and home run odds are before park and pitch adjustments.

How every swing is graded

When you make contact, the game gives the hit a quality from HQ1 (weak) to HQ4 (crushed). It's three pieces added together, capped at 4. A total of 0 or lower is a foul or a miss.

Hit quality = contact cell (−2 to +3) + power roll + skill roll − 1 if power swing
  • Contact cell: where the ball meets the bat on a 7×7 grid around the sweet spot (below). Stance decides which grid you get.
  • Power roll: +1 if a random 0–99 is under the batter's Power. Every swing gets this roll.
  • Skill roll: +1 if a random 0–124 is under Power on a power swing, or under Contact on any other swing.
  • Power swing penalty: −1. So a power swing only reaches HQ4 on a dead-center (+3) contact; a normal swing can get there from a +2.
  • Mouse position doesn't matter. Stance and swing timing are what you control.

The contact grids

Each square is where the ball can meet the bat. Squared keeps the sweet spot in the middle. Open and Closed slide it one column over, which is what you want when the stance matches the pitch.

The game's own debug note: 250 is a home run. Balls launched almost straight up (70–110°) faster than 100 lose a quarter of their speed.

Timing sends the ball

Five timing windows per stance set the direction. 45° is the third-base line and −45° the first-base line for a righty (mirror it for lefties). Past ±45° is foul.

Read the pitcher

  • About 86% of pitches land in the inner half of the pitch circle, 10% in the next quarter, 4% near the edge.
  • Tired pitchers (fatigue near 95+) start throwing wild, and their misses spread past the circle.
  • A repeated pitch type gives the batter +5 speed; the same location as the last pitch gives +15 (power and normal swings).

Pitch costs

  • Cost = base × (1 − (Stamina − 50)/100). Heat base 3, the other three basic pitches 2, special pitches 5–30.
  • 100 Stamina halves every cost; 50 Stamina pays full price.

Foul balls

  • Contact sets the foul rate: roughly 2–5% at 95+ Contact, 40–50% below 30.
  • Low-contact kids wear pitchers down but strike out more.

Every kid, both swings

HR% is the chance a hit clears 250 when you get that contact cell. Power swing pays if tells you how often your contact has to be dead center for the power swing to out-homer the normal swing.
